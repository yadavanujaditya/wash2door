How it works

Four steps, no queue, no carrying bags

  1. Book

    Pick your service and a time slot. The price shows before you confirm.

  2. We collect

    A rider comes to your stay or home, tags your bag and notes anything delicate.

  3. Wash, press, finish

    Each customer's clothes are washed as their own batch, then pressed and checked against the tag.

  4. Delivered folded

    Back at your door in the slot you chose. Pay by UPI or cash when it arrives.

        Questions we hear most

        How long does it take?

        Wash and fold or wash and iron comes back in 24 to 48 hours. Premium care and dry clean take 48 to 72 hours. Choose Express at booking and we aim for 24 hours, for a surcharge of %.

        What does pickup cost?

        Pickup and delivery are free on orders of or more. Smaller orders pay . The minimum order is .

        How do I pay?

        Pay when your clothes are delivered, by UPI or cash. There is nothing to pay online while booking.

        How is the weight worked out?

        You give an estimate when you book. We weigh the bag at pickup in front of you and confirm the final amount before we leave.

        I have delicate or stained clothes.

        Write it in the notes box or tell the rider at pickup. We tag those items and treat them separately. Silks, woollens and sarees are best sent as Premium Care or Dry Clean.

        Something is wrong with my order.

        Call or WhatsApp us the same day it is delivered and we will sort it out with you.

        Which areas do you cover?

        See the list above. If your place is not on it, message us and we will tell you if a rider can reach you.
